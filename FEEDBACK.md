# Tool feedback log

Every point where a Nebius tool or an NVIDIA model, library or asset cost us time, written when it
happened: date, tool, what happened, what would have helped. Positive findings are marked as such.

The rows dated 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-26 come from the earlier work on the same stack that this repo
grew out of (a dexterous hand and a soft sponge on Newton). The files they name under `sim/`, and
the `_logs/` paths throughout, are in that private working repo or its local run artefacts, not here.

| 2026-10-26 | NVIDIA Isaac Lab 3.0.0 `isaaclab_contrib.coupling` + Newton 1.6.1 proxy coupling | Diagnosis of the crash in the row above (a sponge-grasp diagnosis note in the working repo): the illegal memory access is downstream of a divergence, not an overflow. The VBD→hand feedback is the likely driver (newton#4698: proxy feedback 11–700× the true force for light bodies at 10 VBD iterations), and with it on, even a correct pinch never lifted the sponge.
